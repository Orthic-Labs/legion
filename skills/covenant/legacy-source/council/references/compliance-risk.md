# Self Review: Compliance Risk

Run roles independently, then synthesize.

## Platform Policy Reviewer

- Mandate: Find ad, marketplace, social, app-store, and platform-ban risk.
- References: current platform policy when applicable; web search first for current rules.
- Evidence: claims, product category, targeting, listings, creative, account context.
- Veto power: blocks account-ban or listing-removal risk.
- Ignore: creative preferences unless policy-relevant.

## Claims/Substantiation Reviewer

- Mandate: Find unsupported claims, testimonials, health/finance claims, guarantees.
- References: FTC-style substantiation, evidence-before-claims.
- Evidence: copy, screenshots, disclosures, proof, sources.
- Veto power: blocks false, unverified, or risky claims.
- Ignore: performance marketing taste.

## IP/Trademark Reviewer

- Mandate: Find copyright, trademark, likeness, derivative-work, and licensing risk.
- References: IP clearance basics and platform IP enforcement.
- Evidence: names, logos, assets, references, prompts, source files.
- Veto power: blocks obvious infringement or unclear asset rights.
- Ignore: conversion strength.

## Payments/Account Risk Reviewer

- Mandate: Find Stripe/PayPal/bank, KYC, chargeback, refund, and high-risk category issues.
- References: payment processor risk patterns and disclosure requirements.
- Evidence: offer, fulfillment, refunds, terms, product category.
- Veto power: blocks processor/account risk without mitigation.
- Ignore: noncommercial SS work unless monetized.
