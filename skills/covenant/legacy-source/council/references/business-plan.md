# Self Review: Business Plan

Run roles independently, then synthesize.

## Operator

- Mandate: Check execution reality, dependencies, owner load, and sequencing.
- References: operator-first planning, bottleneck thinking, small proof loops.
- Evidence: roadmap, resources, workflow, risks, proof milestones.
- Veto power: blocks plans that cannot be operated with available capacity.
- Ignore: theoretical market appeal without execution path.

## Finance/Unit Economics Reviewer

- Mandate: Check margin, CAC, payback, cash conversion, and pricing assumptions.
- References: unit economics, contribution margin, cashflow discipline.
- Evidence: pricing, costs, channels, conversion assumptions, fulfillment.
- Veto power: blocks plans with impossible economics or missing cost drivers.
- Ignore: brand taste.

## Market Skeptic

- Mandate: Check demand evidence, competition, differentiation, and willingness to pay.
- References: first ten customers, JTBD, Porter tradeoffs.
- Evidence: customer proof, market map, alternatives, wedge.
- Veto power: blocks solution-first plans with no demand proof.
- Ignore: implementation details unless they affect moat.

## Growth/GTM Reviewer

- Mandate: Check channel fit, distribution loops, launch path, and retention.
- References: channel-market fit, compounding distribution, proof before scale.
- Evidence: acquisition plan, activation, retention, referrals, content/ads.
- Veto power: blocks "build it and they come" plans.
- Ignore: backend architecture.

## Risk Inversion Reviewer

- Mandate: Ask how this fails and what would make it obvious early.
- References: Munger inversion, premortem, reversible bets.
- Evidence: assumptions, constraints, kill criteria, rollback.
- Veto power: blocks plans without riskiest assumption or stop rule.
- Ignore: optimism unless unsupported.
