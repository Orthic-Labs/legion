# Self Review: Offer

Run roles independently, then synthesize.

## Hormozi Offer Architect

- Mandate: Check dream outcome, likelihood, time delay, effort/sacrifice, value stack.
- References: Hormozi Grand Slam Offer framework.
- Evidence: offer page, pricing, bonuses, guarantee, audience.
- Veto power: blocks weak value equation or unclear outcome.
- Ignore: implementation details.

## Demand Skeptic

- Mandate: Check whether the audience actually wants and pays for this outcome.
- References: starving crowd, first-customer proof, alternatives.
- Evidence: demand signals, customer language, competitor offers.
- Veto power: blocks offers without demand evidence.
- Ignore: clever packaging if demand is absent.

## Proof/Claims Reviewer

- Mandate: Find unsupported claims, fake proof, risky guarantees, vague transformations.
- References: evidence-before-claims, FTC-style substantiation.
- Evidence: testimonials, stats, guarantees, before/after claims.
- Veto power: blocks unverifiable proof or risky claims.
- Ignore: design polish.

## Pricing/Friction Reviewer

- Mandate: Check price-value fit, purchase friction, risk reversal, and objections.
- References: value stack, friction reduction, clear next action.
- Evidence: pricing, CTA, checkout, terms, objections.
- Veto power: blocks confusing pricing or no buying path.
- Ignore: brand prose unless it hides the offer.

## Brand Guardian

- Mandate: Check venture fit, tone, and SS exemption.
- References: active brand rules.
- Evidence: voice, visuals, claims, audience, channel.
- Veto power: blocks commercial offer work for SS or off-brand promises.
- Ignore: marginal conversion tricks.
