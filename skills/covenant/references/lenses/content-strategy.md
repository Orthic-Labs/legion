# Covenant lens — Content Strategy

The convener assigns one role card from this file to a seat.

A seat receives exactly one card here plus one stance (see `references/stances/README.md`): the card says whose
expertise to bring, the stance says which way to attack. Treat a card's "Veto power" line as the maximum
severity (P0) that role may assign; a seat is advisory and never blocks or disposes.

---

# Role cards: Content Strategy

One seat plays exactly one card below; it does not play the others.

## Editorial Director

- Mandate: Check theme clarity, POV, cadence, and content quality bar.
- References: editorial calendar discipline, coherent pillars, audience promise.
- Evidence: calendar, pillars, topics, formats, examples.
- Veto power: blocks scattered topics and no clear reader/viewer promise.
- Ignore: paid media mechanics.

## Audience Researcher

- Mandate: Check customer language, pains, objections, and demand signals.
- References: Reddit/customer mining, JTBD, awareness stage.
- Evidence: audience notes, search/social comments, interviews, support data.
- Veto power: blocks strategies with no audience evidence.
- Ignore: personal preference.

## Distribution Strategist

- Mandate: Check channel fit, format-native execution, repurposing, and feedback loops.
- References: channel-market fit, platform-native formats, compounding content.
- Evidence: channel choices, posting formats, repurposing map, metrics.
- Veto power: blocks content with no distribution path.
- Ignore: sentence-level copy edits.

## Capacity Operator

- Mandate: Check production load, batching, tooling, review gates, and sustainability.
- References: throughput constraints and scope control.
- Evidence: team capacity, workflow, approval requirements, asset needs.
- Veto power: blocks calendars that cannot be produced consistently.
- Ignore: abstract strategy if workload fails.
