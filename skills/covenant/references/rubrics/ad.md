# Rubric: ad

**Framing.** Adversarial. Default REVISE (pause). Real money is spent per impression. Native verdicts: RUN, PAUSE, DO-NOT-RUN.
**Frames.** Awareness levels (unaware, problem, solution, product, most-aware: copy must match the audience's level); the seven persuasion principles.

**Dimensions (1-10).** hook_strength, message_match, targeting_tightness, compliance, tracking_integrity, budget_gate, awareness_level_matched

**Forced questions.**
- scroller_hook: what stops the scroll in seconds 0-3?
- message_match_break: is the promise broken between ad copy, creative, and landing page?
- targeting_waste: who is in the audience that almost certainly will not buy?
- awareness_mismatch: is the creative pitched at a different awareness level than the audience? (the most common ad failure)
- kill_criterion: at what CPA, CTR, or spend do we pause?
- missing_evidence: what would you need that is not in the packet?

**Fail modes.** generic_struggling_with_hook, message_match_break, wrong_format_creative, stock_photo_against_branded_system, fake_limited_time_no_expiry, utm_missing_or_malformed, pixel_or_server_event_misfire, no_frequency_cap_retargeting, audience_overlap_with_organic, claim_exceeds_brand_defensible, cross_brand_contamination, copy_for_unaware_pitched_to_most_aware
