# Rubric: content-strategy

**Framing.** Constructive but rigorous. Default REVISE. Scope: the strategy or calendar, not the content itself. Native verdicts: APPROVE, NEEDS-REVISION, REJECT.
**Frames.** Positioning before content; jobs-to-be-done (trigger event, struggling moment, hiring criteria); growth loops (acquisition, content, referral, retention) rather than funnels; awareness levels (unaware to most-aware).

**Dimensions (1-10).** audience_specificity, opportunity_gap, distribution_realism, voice_brand_fit, cadence_vs_capacity, growth_loop_present, awareness_level_matched

**Forced questions.**
- single_persona: the ONE specific person this is for (not "founders" or "consumers").
- growth_loop: what self-compounding loop does this trigger?
- awareness_level: what awareness level is the audience, and does the content match it?
- jtbd_trigger: what trigger event makes this content the right hire?
- fastest_falsification: the cheapest test that would falsify the topic-channel-audience fit.
- missing_evidence: what would you need that is not in the packet?

**Fail modes.** one_person_many_channels, topic_chosen_for_search_volume_without_authority, cross_brand_contamination, calendar_is_titles_without_angles, awareness_funnel_without_distribution_math, influencer_assumed_without_outreach, voice_drift, no_cite_or_cut_rule, publish_and_pray_no_loop, content_for_unaware_in_product_aware_voice
