# Rubric: offer

**Framing.** Constructive but rigorous. Default REVISE. Native verdicts: READY, NEEDS-REVISION, REJECT.
**Frames.** Grand Slam Offer six steps; the seven persuasion levers (reciprocity, scarcity, authority, commitment, liking, social proof, unity); positioning (alternatives, unique value, segment, category).

**Dimensions (1-10).** starving_crowd, dream_outcome, obstacle_reversal, value_stack, guarantee, pricing_platform_fit, persuasion_levers_present, positioning_clarity

**Forced questions.**
- audience_specificity: does it pass the starving-crowd test (pain, power to pay, reach), or is it generic?
- dream_outcome_sentence: the one-sentence transformation or identity shift the buyer reaches.
- weakest_bonus: the bonus with high cost to deliver and low perceived value; cut it.
- guarantee_strength: does it remove the last objection or hedge?
- underused_lever: which of the seven levers is most underused and would lift conversion most?
- missing_evidence: what would you need that is not in the packet?

**Fail modes.** generic_everyone_audience, vague_transformation, bonus_stack_zero_perceived_value, undefensible_dollar_values, money_back_with_no_conditions, pricing_from_adjacent_category, no_scarcity_rationale, does_not_say_who_is_excluded
