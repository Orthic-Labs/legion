# Control-plane budgets

**Stage:** S02 · **Ledger:** S02-05 · **Owner:** Legion + Arcane doctrine. This bounds machinery; it creates no runtime budget tree.

Hooks, subagent trees, worker batches, waits/monitors, recovery loops, & subprocesses spend parent objective-lineage envelope. Before admission each declares finite wall time, active time, count, concurrency, nesting, & spawn-depth limits. A child may receive no more remaining budget than parent & may not mint a lineage.

Repeated gate blocks consume one calibrated consecutive-block ceiling then terminate into typed machinery-defect/recovery, not repeated identical gate invocation. Missing limit, reset attempt, or child expansion rejects that work. Exact calibrated values, counters, & termination actions belong to one versioned capability/calibration table plus S09 conformance, not this doctrine.
