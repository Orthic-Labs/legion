# Machinery-defect isolation

**Stage:** S02 · **Ledger:** S02-08 · **Owner:** Legion + Arcane doctrine. S05/S09 own concrete gate recovery & enforcement.

When assurance/control machinery fails, record separate `OUT_OF_SCOPE_MACHINERY_DEFECT` with affected evidence & safety consequences. Use sanctioned degradation, alternate evidence path, or recoverable delivery route; continue next unmet frozen required item.

Repair machinery in current delivery only where failure invalidates required outcome evidence or safety. A gate may deny unsafe effect; it cannot silently replace product task. Recovery path is narrow, independently authenticated, & not dependent on failed ordinary control plane. This control does not authorize broad gate debugging, runtime repair, or scope expansion.
