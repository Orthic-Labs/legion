# Simplicity & YAGNI review

Use after a candidate is selected or materially changed. Inputs: selected mechanisms, driving scenarios/invariants, existing boundaries, distribution boundaries, & lifecycle complexity count. Outputs: remove/inherit/retain decision per mechanism, paid distribution-tax record, & minimum-sufficient result.

Do not use to erase a known safety, public-contract, irreversible-data, or authority risk. Remove mechanisms without drivers; inherit existing mechanisms; add a boundary only when a named driver proves an existing boundary cannot absorb it. Every distributed boundary pays for partial failure, latency, consistency, delivery semantics, operations, security, debugging, & coordination.
