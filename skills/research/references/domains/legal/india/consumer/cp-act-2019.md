# Consumer Protection Act, 2019 — Section Map

Working reference for the sections that recur in District Commission consumer complaints. **This is a memory aid, not the bare act.** Every entry is tagged. Anything `[UNVERIFIED]` must be confirmed against the bare act at **indiacode.nic.in** (Act 35 of 2019) before it goes into a draft, a pleading, or advice.

`[VERIFIED 2026-05-14]` = confirmed via web search on that date. `[UNVERIFIED]` = commonly cited and very likely correct, but not independently confirmed this build — confirm before relying.

## Definitions (Section 2)

| Section | Term | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2(7) | **Consumer** | `[VERIFIED 2026-05-14]` | A person who buys goods / avails services for consideration. **Excludes** goods/services obtained for resale or commercial purpose — **but** the explanation preserves a person who buys/avails exclusively for earning a livelihood by self-employment. This livelihood/self-employment carve-out is the basis for a business-paid claim. Confirm exact sub-clause numbering (e.g. 2(7)(ii)) against the bare act when citing it precisely. |
| 2(6) | **Complainant** | `[UNVERIFIED]` | Who may file — consumer, registered consumer association, Central/State Govt, etc. Confirm. |
| 2(11) | **Deficiency** | `[UNVERIFIED]` | Any fault, imperfection, shortcoming or inadequacy in the quality, nature and manner of performance of a service. Commonly cited as the core ground for non-delivery / non-performance. Confirm number. |
| 2(28) | **Misleading advertisement** | `[UNVERIFIED]` | An advertisement that falsely describes a product/service, gives a false guarantee, conveys an express/implied representation that would be an unfair trade practice, or deliberately conceals important information. Confirm number. |
| 2(42) | **Service** | `[UNVERIFIED]` | Service of any description made available to potential users. Note: the Act defines *service*, not "service provider" — drafting that says "service provider within the meaning of S.2(42)" is loose; tie the OP to the *service* definition or describe it as a person providing a service. Confirm number. |
| 2(47) | **Unfair trade practice** | `[UNVERIFIED]` | A trade practice that adopts unfair/deceptive methods to promote sale/use/supply — false representation, bait, non-issue of bills, etc. Confirm number. |
| 2(9) | **Consumer rights** | `[UNVERIFIED]` | Six rights. Rarely cited directly in pleadings. Confirm. |

## Jurisdiction, filing, procedure, limitation

| Section | Subject | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 34 | **Jurisdiction of the District Commission** | `[VERIFIED 2026-05-14]` | 34(1) pecuniary — value of consideration paid does not exceed ₹50,00,000 (post-2021 amendment). 34(2) territorial — complaint may be instituted where an OP resides/carries on business/has a branch/works for gain, OR where the cause of action arose wholly or in part, OR **where the complainant resides or personally works for gain**. The complainant-residence limb is what makes party-in-person filing convenient. See `jurisdiction-and-fees.md`. |
| 35 | **Manner in which complaint shall be made** | `[UNVERIFIED]` | The section under which a consumer complaint to the District Commission is filed. Prior drafted packs cite "Section 35" as the filing section throughout. Confirm it is 35 (and not 36, which concerns proceedings) against the bare act. |
| 38 | **Procedure on admission of complaint** | `[UNVERIFIED]` | How the District Commission proceeds — referral to opposite party, analysis, etc. Confirm. |
| 39 | **Findings of the District Commission** | `[UNVERIFIED]` | The reliefs the Commission can order — refund, removal of deficiency, compensation, discontinuance of unfair trade practice, etc. The "Prayer" should map to reliefs the Commission can actually grant under this section. Confirm. |
| 69 | **Limitation period** | `[VERIFIED 2026-05-14]` | A complaint must be filed within **two years from the date on which the cause of action arose**. The Commission may condone delay for sufficient cause, recorded in writing. A continuing cause of action keeps the window open. |
| 71 | **Enforcement of orders** | `[UNVERIFIED]` | How District Commission orders are enforced. Confirm. |
| 72 | **Penalty for non-compliance** | `[UNVERIFIED]` | Consequence if an OP does not comply with an order. Confirm. |

## How to use this map

1. When a draft cites a section, find it here. If it is `[VERIFIED]`, proceed. If `[UNVERIFIED]`, run a WebSearch against indiacode.nic.in or a reliable legal source and confirm the number and the substance **before** the citation goes into a pleading.
2. Prefer pleading the **substance** ("the opposite party received consideration but rendered no service, which is a deficiency in service") over leaning on a bare number. A correct description with a slightly-off section number is recoverable; a confident wrong number in an affidavit is not.
3. The 2019 Act repealed and replaced the Consumer Protection Act, 1986. Old-Act section numbers (e.g. the 1986 Act's S.12) do not apply — do not mix them in.
4. Pecuniary slabs were changed by notification dated **30 December 2021**. Any pre-2022 source quoting ₹1 crore for the District Commission is stale.
