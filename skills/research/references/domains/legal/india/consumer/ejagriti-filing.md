# e-Jagriti Filing Workflow

How a District Commission consumer complaint actually gets onto the record. Portal specifics change — anything not `[VERIFIED]` must be confirmed live on the portal before you walk a user through it.

`[OBSERVED 2026-05]` = seen directly during the May 2026 filings (reliable but portal UI can change). `[UNVERIFIED]` = general knowledge, confirm live.

---

## Portals

| Portal | URL | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **NCH** — National Consumer Helpline | consumerhelpline.gov.in | Pre-litigation grievance / mediation. Produces a **docket number**. **Not a forum** — it cannot pass a binding order. Use it to build the record, never as a substitute for filing. |
| **e-Jagriti** (the e-Daakhil successor) | e-jagriti.gov.in | The actual filing portal for District / State / National consumer commissions. Binding adjudication happens here. |

## Before you touch the portal

1. **NCH grievance** filed → docket number saved (it becomes an annexure).
2. **Pre-litigation notice** sent to every OP — email and/or registered post — with an explicit working-day cure period. Save the sent copy; note the date the cure period expires. This becomes an annexure and the most recent "cause of action" event.
3. **e-Jagriti account** created; complainant profile complete (it auto-populates the complainant fields at filing time). `[OBSERVED 2026-05]`
4. The **six-document pack** generated, the legal substance reviewed, the Complaint+Affidavit **notarised**, and **every document plus every annexure converted to PDF**. e-Jagriti takes PDF uploads.

## The new-case form — step by step `[OBSERVED 2026-05]`

Path observed: `e-jagriti.gov.in/consumer/case-filing/new-case-form`

1. **Case Type** → *Consumer Complaint (CC)*, *Normal Filing*.
2. **Case Details** →
   - Amount paid as consideration (e.g. ₹18,600)
   - Total claim value (e.g. ₹50,000)
   - State (e.g. Maharashtra), District (e.g. Mumbai Suburban)
   - Case Category / Sub Category / Sub-Sub Category — pick the closest fit (May 2026 example: *Service Deficiency* → *Service Provider Fraud or Misrepresentation* → *Service Scams*). The category tree is the portal's, not the Act's — choose the nearest, don't agonise.
3. **Complainant details** — auto-populated from the account profile; review and save.
4. **Additional Complainant** — skip if sole complainant.
5. **Opposite Party 1** — name, address, contact. Fill from the Memo of Parties.
6. **Additional Opposite Party** — add OP-2 (and beyond) here, one block each — e.g. a director named personally, or a second company.
7. **Document Upload** — upload the pack and annexures as PDFs (see naming below).
8. **Review & Submit** — submit. The registry then assigns the **Consumer Complaint Number** — the blank left in every document. Note it once issued.

> A part-completed case can be saved as a draft. Drafts may expire — if you return and the draft is gone, the case has to be re-entered. Don't start the portal form until the PDFs are ready to upload in one sitting.

## What to upload `[OBSERVED 2026-05 + UNVERIFIED ordering]`

Upload as PDFs, in the Index order:

1. Index
2. Proforma for Filing Consumer Complaint
3. Synopsis with List of Dates and Events
4. Memo of Parties
5. Consumer Complaint under Section 35 + Verification + **notarised** Affidavit
6. Party-in-Person Declaration (if filing without an advocate)
7. Annexures A-1, A-2, … in the order the Index lists them

Confirm on the portal whether it wants one combined PDF or separate files per item — this has varied. Name files so the registry can read the bundle in order, e.g. `01_Index.pdf`, `02_Proforma.pdf`, … `A-01_<desc>.pdf`.

## Annexure discipline

- The Index lists annexures A-1 onward. **Every listed annexure must exist as a real, scannable PDF** before filing — do not list evidence you do not hold.
- The Affidavit swears the annexures are "true and faithful copies of their respective originals." A native digital original (a video, an email) should be described accurately in the Affidavit (e.g. "filed in its native digital form / original recording"), not claimed as a photocopy.
- Keep the annexure list in the Index, the count in the Affidavit, and the actual uploaded files in lockstep. The generator derives the Affidavit's "A-1 to A-N" range from the Index — if you add an annexure, regenerate; don't hand-edit.

## After filing

- Registry assigns the Consumer Complaint Number → record it; it fills the blank in the cause title.
- The Commission issues notice to the opposite parties.
- Track hearing dates; a party-in-person must be available for every listed date (the Party-in-Person Declaration commits the complainant to this).

## Common portal mistakes

- Starting the new-case form before the PDFs are ready, then losing the draft.
- Uploading the un-notarised Affidavit.
- Mismatch between annexures listed in the Index and files actually uploaded.
- Picking a category tree path and treating it as a legal characterisation — it is just the portal's routing taxonomy.
- Forgetting to record the assigned complaint number, so later documents still show the blank.
