# Legal domain

Resolve country, legal area, and issue before a jurisdictional conclusion. Do not default to India
merely because the request sounds legal.

Legal areas include consumer, criminal, civil, contract, employment, corporate, tax, IP, privacy,
family, immigration, regulatory, and other. Add forum, region, posture, side, dates, documents, and
desired outcome when material.

## Authority model

Legal evidence records authority type, forum, citation, effective/decision date, precedential
status, negative treatment, current-as-of date, pinpoint, and official source. Search results and
commentary are leads. High-confidence legal rules require current primary authority; high-confidence
case-law additionally requires the judgment and current-status verification.

## Matter isolation and output gates

Keep matter facts scoped to the run. Prefer user forms, precedents, playbooks, and documents before
outside research. Separate supplied facts, assumptions, disputed facts, and missing evidence.
Drafting support may produce a review draft, but sending, signing, filing, notarising, accepting, or
relying requires the route's human gate. Do not claim representation, privilege, or attorney status.

India consumer commission is a nested workflow. India criminal is a distinct nested workflow with
consumer paths mechanically forbidden.
