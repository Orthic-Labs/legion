# Assurance

Assurance is independent from scale.

| Level | Required contract |
|---|---|
| `quick` | Triage answer; load-bearing price/feature/legal/benchmark claims still require an opened primary source. |
| `standard` | Evidence/claim ledger, automatic independence clustering, contradiction view, and citation support check. |
| `verified` | Standard + overturn/gap review, domain verifier, fresh DOI retraction sweep, final citation support check, patch-only receipt, and ship receipt. |

`verified` is a stronger evidence process, not a synonym for a large report. A focused medical or
legal question can be verified. A broad landscape can remain standard.
