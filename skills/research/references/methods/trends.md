# Method — trends

Freeze the topic, geography, platforms, comparison window, and decision. Use the normal Research
providers; do not invoke another skill or import a provider-specific prompt body.

1. Search at least two signal classes when available: primary releases/changelogs, community
   discussion, independent reporting, search interest, marketplace/review activity, or public data.
2. Record publication/event dates and distinguish a new event from an old item resurfacing.
3. Collapse cross-posts, syndicated reporting, repeated press releases, and the same creator posting
   across platforms into one independence cluster.
4. Separate volume from growth, and growth from durable adoption. One viral thread is an anecdote,
   not a trend.
5. Preserve opposing and downvoted views; actively search for falsifying evidence rather than mining
   only for validation.
6. Render each signal with scope, evidence IDs, confidence, and what would overturn it. Mark platform
   bias and sampling gaps.

A search result or social summary is a lead until the source/thread is opened and the relevant
passage located. Use `verified` assurance when a public or commercial decision depends on the trend.
