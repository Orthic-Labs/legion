# Method — search discipline

Search effort is governed by the frozen route and hook-metered budget, not by a fixed source quota.

1. Decompose the decision into must-answer atomic questions and define what evidence would close
   each one.
2. Search narrowly first. A search hit is a lead; load-bearing claims require `open` plus a located
   passage before ledger admission.
3. Prefer proposition-fit original sources: official pages for current self-reported facts,
   statutes/regulators/judgments for legal rules, primary studies and registries for scientific or
   medical effects, and independent measurements for comparisons.
4. Chase citations and contradictions only when they close a recorded gap. Repeated reformulations
   that yield no new opened evidence are search drift and trigger stopping or a typed degradation.
5. Stop when coverage is met, the budget is exhausted, or two consecutive targeted searches add no
   material evidence. Record omissions rather than widening silently.
6. Respect source independence: syndicated copies and reports of one origin count as one voice.

## Quotation limits

Default to paraphrase. Keep direct quotations under 15 words, use at most one direct quote per
source in the rendered artifact, and retain a locator for verification. Never reproduce song lyrics,
poems, or other short complete works.
