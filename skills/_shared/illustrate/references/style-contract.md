# Illustration Style Contract

## Canonical reference

Use `../assets/style-anchor.png`, approved 2026-08-02, as the style anchor. Match its medium, line quality, material restraint, tonal density, & semantic clarity. Do not copy its specific composition unless the concept is selection through a membrane.

## Core idea

Depict software or systems as living structures with visible, mechanically credible control. Biology supplies organization & adaptation; mechanics supplies action & causality. Result should feel like a field-guide plate crossed with an engineering cutaway, drawn by hand on matte stock.

## Rendering language

- Use precise graphite & pen-and-ink contours with fine cross-hatching.
- Add restrained colored-pencil & flat gouache washes; preserve dry paper tooth.
- Keep edges hand-drawn, slightly irregular, & controlled.
- Use shallow pictorial depth: layered cutaway or side elevation, not cinematic 3D perspective.
- Render metal as graphite, dull steel, or oxidized mineral—not chrome.
- Render biological structures as parchment, plant fiber, cell wall, cartilage, mycelium, mineralized tissue, or translucent dry membrane—not wet flesh.
- Favor charcoal grounds, warm ivory, smoke blue-gray, graphite, & one product accent.
- Keep light diffuse & illustrative. Avoid glossy highlights, bloom, lens effects, & volumetric haze.

## Semantic grammar

Build each image from four roles:

1. **Input:** tangled, noisy, abundant, fragmented, or unrefined material.
2. **Living structure:** membrane, branching network, cellular lattice, shell, root, tendon, leaf vein, or layered tissue that organizes flow.
3. **Mechanism:** valve, cam, ratchet, spring, bearing, hinge, guide, clamp, shuttle, iris, pulley, sieve, or tensioner with a visible job.
4. **Outcome or agency:** one selected path, clean output, repaired structure, controlled release, or hand performing a deliberate action.

Use only roles needed by the concept. Avoid symbolic filler.

Do not ask viewers to decode private symbolism such as one shape meaning a claim, another meaning evidence, or a leaf meaning code. Show a familiar physical action first; let nearby copy connect that action to the product.

## Mechanical truth

- Show what moves, what stays fixed, & where force transfers.
- Seat screws, pivots, springs, cables, & guides in believable mountings.
- Use an iris only for aperture or variable opening; use a pinch valve for constriction; use a ratchet for one-way progress; use a sieve for separation.
- Prefer one understandable mechanism over several decorative ones.
- Never place rocks, beads, pods, rings, or ornaments inside machinery unless their function is explicit.

## Composition

- Establish one readable left-to-right, bottom-to-top, or center-out action.
- Use density contrast: complex input, legible mechanism, calm outcome.
- Keep one dominant focal point & one secondary action.
- Leave deliberate dark or paper-toned copy space for website layouts.
- Crop with confidence but keep cause, mechanism, & effect visible.
- Exclude generated typography, logos, labels, UI, & watermarks unless explicitly requested.

## Color behavior

- Use one accent for meaning, never decoration.
- Keep accent coverage below roughly 10% of the frame.
- Apply the accent to selected material, active force, current route, or transformed result.
- For Orthic Labs, inherit the product accent; use ember `#DF6428` only when no product accent is defined.
- For Right Suite, read the app's locked light/dark tokens in `/workspace/.claude/rules/brands.md` & substitute its accent. Do not homogenize app palettes.

## Human presence

- Include a hand when the promise is selection, control, rescue, repair, extraction, or direct manipulation.
- Draw hands anatomically, calmly, & purposefully.
- Make contact visible: grip, tension, pressure, rotation, release, or placement.
- Avoid severed-hand staging, medical-procedure framing, restraint, or ambiguous tethering.

## Hard negatives

Reject photorealism, glossy CGI, wet tissue, exposed viscera, fetus or embryo imagery, eggs or sacs, tumors, alien pods, body horror, slime, mucous sheen, biomechanical horror, steampunk ornament, random gears, decorative wires, unexplained stones, camera-aperture clichés, tethered baubles, floating symbols, neon, lasers, cyberpunk glow, generic SaaS vectors, stock 3D blobs, plastic surfaces, fake depth-of-field, text, logos, & watermarks.

## Family use

Treat this as a shared illustration language, not a replacement identity:

- Keep Orthic Labs concepts biological, systemic, infrastructural, & research-like.
- Keep Right Suite concepts task-specific & human-centered; translate each app's real action into semantic grammar.
- Preserve locked logos, icon packs, wordmarks, fonts, UI tokens, & product screenshots.
- Change subject, metaphor, mechanism, layout, & accent by product. Reuse only medium, line language, material handling, & semantic discipline.
