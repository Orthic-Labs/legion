# Offer design

Use this branch only for a named venture, validated product, target buyer, channel, & known price or price range. Load `/brand <venture>` when available. Never apply commercial tooling to Stunning Strangers.

## Method

1. Define one reachable buyer with urgent pain, purchasing power, & one desired outcome.
2. State desired functional, emotional, & status outcomes.
3. List barriers across perceived success likelihood, delay, effort, sacrifice, & trust.
4. Convert each barrier into a solution plus smallest credible delivery vehicle.
5. Remove expensive low-value components. Group retained components into core offer & bonuses. Use only defensible values.
6. Draft unconditional, conditional, & service-based guarantees. Recommend one with explicit risk.

Return buyer, outcome, ranked barriers, solution/delivery map, offer stack, price logic, & three guarantees as one brief. Route finished sales copy to `writing`.

Do not invent proof, demand, values, urgency, scarcity, outcomes, or guarantee terms. Validate missing market facts through `research` before construction.
