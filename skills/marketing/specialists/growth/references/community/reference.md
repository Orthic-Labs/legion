# Community-Led Growth

Use this when the growth lever is people talking to each other, not just the brand publishing at them.

## Strategy Questions

1. What identity does the community reinforce?
2. What value do members get before the company gets anything?
3. What action should a healthy member take weekly?
4. What business job should the community support: retention, activation, referrals, support, research, or credibility?
5. Who will own rituals, moderation, and follow-up?

## Core Loop

```text
Members join -> get value -> participate -> create/help -> other people discover -> members join
```

Design every channel, ritual, and incentive around accelerating the loop.

## Launch From Zero

1. Recruit 20-50 founding members manually.
2. Define the culture in behavior terms: what great participation looks like.
3. Seed 5-10 example posts before opening the door.
4. Reply to every post at first.
5. Create one weekly ritual members can remember.

## Growth / Audit

Track:

- New member post rate within 7 days
- Thread reply rate
- DAU/MAU or weekly active ratio
- Posts by non-staff
- Questions unanswered after 24 hours
- Concentration risk: whether the same few members create almost everything

Fixes:

- Low posting: improve onboarding prompt, ask easier first question.
- Low replies: assign hosts, tag relevant people, seed examples.
- Low retention: add recurring rituals and member wins.
- Low business value: connect rituals to product use cases, customer proof, or referrals.

## Output Formats

- Community strategy doc
- Channel architecture
- New-member journey
- Ritual calendar
- Advocate/ambassador brief
- Health audit with top 3 fixes
