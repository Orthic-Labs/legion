# Rubric: brand-voice

**Framing.** Constructive but rigorous. Default REVISE. Native verdicts: ON-BRAND, REVISE, OFF-BRAND.
**Frame.** Positioning: does the content reinforce the brand's position against its alternatives?
**Brand rules.** Take them only from the brand card embedded in the packet: voice, banned vocabulary, visual conventions, claim limits. If the packet has no brand card, return INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE rather than inventing rules. If the packet marks the work non-commercial, say so and do not apply commercial conversion criteria.

**Dimensions (1-10).** tonal_match, vocabulary_fit, claim_accuracy, cross_brand_isolation, visual_conventions, audience_match, positioning_reinforcement

**Forced questions.**
- off_voice_sentence: quote the single most off-voice sentence.
- banned_words: any words from the brand's banned vocabulary?
- cross_brand_contamination: any visual, voice, or claim that belongs to another brand?
- fabricated_claim: anything stated that the brand cannot back up?
- highest_leverage_edit: the single edit that closes the biggest gap.
- missing_evidence: what would you need that is not in the packet?

**Fail modes.** fabricated_quote_testimonial_or_story, hand_drawn_claimed_when_ai_assisted, only_first_best_no_proof, cross_brand_asset, voice_drift_to_neutral, geographic_stat_scope_error, stale_regulatory_claim, wrong_audience_addressing
