# Rubric: blogs

**Framing.** Constructive but rigorous. Default REVISE. Scope: blog drafts and published posts, including content quality, E-E-A-T, and AI-citation readiness. Native verdicts: PUBLISH, REVISE, REJECT.

**Dimensions (1-10).** hook_strength, voice_fidelity, factual_accuracy, info_density, structure, action_clarity, eeat_signals, ai_citation_readiness

**Forced questions.**
- bounce_point: the paragraph or moment where the average reader closes the tab.
- claim_to_verify: the most specific claim or statistic that needs verification before publishing (cite and scope).
- voice_drift: a specific phrase that drifts from the brand voice, if one is specified.
- ai_default_tendency: one generic-AI tell (mid-sentence em dashes, "it is worth noting", reflexive three-item lists, hedging).
- reader_next_action: the explicit or implicit call to action; what does the reader do next?
- missing_evidence: what would you need that is not in the packet?

**E-E-A-T checks.** Experience: first-hand use and lived examples rather than research summary. Expertise: credentials, primary sources, technical accuracy. Authoritativeness: authoritative links, consistent subject matter. Trust: citations, byline, date, contact, no fabricated statistics.
**AI-citation readiness.** Scannable headings, lists, and tables; short self-contained passages that answer specific questions; defined terms, FAQ blocks, and steps; specific data, dates, and attributable claims; a point of view only this source has.

**Fail modes.** listicle_padding, stat_no_source_no_scope, ai_hedging, voice_mismatch_brand, stale_regulatory, headers_do_not_match_section, internal_contradiction, missing_target_keyword_in_open, in_conclusion_wrap, generic_open, no_first_hand_experience, no_primary_sources, no_byline_no_date, no_quotable_passages, rephrased_competitor_content
