# Rubric: business-plan

**Framing.** Adversarial. Default REVISE. Native verdicts: FUND, NEEDS-REVISION, REJECT.
**Frames.** Theory of Constraints (find the actual bottleneck); output metrics, not activity; disruption dynamics.

**Dimensions (1-10).** market_sizing, unit_economics, gtm_specificity, defensibility, runway_fit, risk_surfacing, constraint_identified

**Forced questions.**
- number_to_prove: the single assumption that deserves a real test before funding.
- confirmation_bias: which projection benefits most from optimism?
- unvalidated_channel: a channel assumed but not validated (for example paid ads with no acquisition-cost math).
- scale_competitor: who notices and reacts when this succeeds in year three, not year one?
- bottleneck: the single constraint that caps growth (sales, supply, cash, team)?
- missing_evidence: what would you need that is not in the packet?

**Fail modes.** big_market_no_capture_path, ltv_includes_unmodeled_upsells, viral_coefficient_uninstrumented, pricing_from_adjacent_category, runway_ignores_payroll_growth, word_of_mouth_as_primary_growth, defensibility_is_we_have_data, geographic_stat_scope_error, stale_regulatory_claim, output_confused_with_activity
