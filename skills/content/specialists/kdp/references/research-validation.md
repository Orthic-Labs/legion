## Research Checklist

- Search Amazon for seed and 3-5 long-tail keywords.
- Record top 10 organic books: BSR/sales proxy, review count, price, format, pub date.
- Read low-star reviews from top 3 competitors.
- Check Etsy if printable/activity pages are plausible.
- Check if buyers expect paperback, printable PDF, bundle, classroom pack, or gift book.
- Confirm trim norms by category: kids coloring/activity often 8.5 x 11; journals often 6 x 9; adult coloring usually 8.5 x 11.
- Check policy/sensitivity before building: religion, kids, violence, nudity, medical/yoga claims, AI content.

## Source Classes

For mythology/yoga books, use a source ladder:

1. Primary or tradition-rooted story source when available.
2. Reliable summaries such as Britannica, Britannica Kids, Hindu American Foundation, museum/university resources, or recognized cultural organizations.
3. Production notes that clearly say where the retelling is adapted, softened, or interpretive.

If no source is verified, do not present the claim as scripture. Mark it as an open source check.

## Go Signals

- Real buyer query exists.
- Competitors sell but leave quality gaps.
- Review counts are not impossible to overcome.
- Product is differentiated by story, cultural care, format, art quality, or series.
- the operator has credibility or taste advantage.
- The book can pass KDP quality and policy checks.

## No-Go Signals

- Pure trend-chasing with no the operator edge.
- Generic AI coloring pages with no teaching layer.
- Repetitive low-content interior.
- High-risk religious/violent/sexual imagery for the target age.
- Keyword is saturated by polished incumbents and the product has no sharper angle.
- Research relies on vibes, not observed listings/reviews/search behavior.
