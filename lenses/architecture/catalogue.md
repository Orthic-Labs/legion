# Architecture lens catalogue

This is an omission scan, not a mandatory checklist. Admit a lens only when a frozen driver, scenario, constraint, risk, or decision makes it material; record applicability, inputs, outputs, & negative scope.

Scan: modularity, functional fit, performance, scalability, reliability, interaction, data, security, privacy, deployment, operations, distribution, organization, ownership, mission, economics, vendor, safety, interoperability, evolvability, uncertainty, & complexity.
