# Reliability & operations lens

**Applicability:** continuity, recovery, deployment, observability, capacity, or operational ownership is material. **Inputs:** top failure scenario, SLO/constraint, topology, recovery path, & operator owner. **Outputs:** failure story, recovery/operability requirements, evidence plan, & residual-risk owner. **Negative scope:** no theoretical availability claim, generic monitoring list, or operational redesign without a driving scenario.
