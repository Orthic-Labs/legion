# Control lifecycle & retirement

**Stage:** S02 · **Ledger:** S02-09 · **Owner:** Arcane control doctrine. S09 owns enforcement; S12 owns calibration/retirement outcome review.

Every control has one canonical owner & status `PROPOSED | ACTIVE | DEPRECATED | RETIRED | SUPERSEDED`, plus rationale, live acceptance/safety obligations, live consumers, replacement/supersession, migration plan, schema/eval/metric/doc updates, retirement evidence, conformance result, & change time.

`RETIRED` or `SUPERSEDED` requires migrated empty live obligations & consumers plus fresh conformance evidence. `DEPRECATED` is transitional, never permanent parking. Silent deletion, consumer-stranding, missing obligation proof, or missing migration/update evidence rejects retirement. Canonical owner changes follow canon-map proof; this control defines no registry or deletion action.
