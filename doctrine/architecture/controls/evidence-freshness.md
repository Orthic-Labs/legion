# Evidence freshness

**Stage:** S02 · **Ledger:** S02-08 · **Owner:** Legion + Arcane doctrine. S05 owns evidence registry/seal consumers; S09 computes/enforces lifecycle.

Completion evidence names verification run, observation time, exact integrated-state identity, acceptance fingerprint, method, freshness basis, validity horizon, & `FRESH | STALE | EXPIRED | STATE_MISMATCH`. S05 evaluates this against acceptance registry artifacts; it postdates latest material change to observed surface. State, method, validity, commit, or acceptance drift invalidates prior closure evidence; stale proof leaves outcome `CANDIDATE`.

Milestones, patches, unit/API/proxy/offscreen output, producer claims, & worker attestation are candidate evidence only. Required closure needs observed acceptance-surface proof for exact integrated state. Durable evidence stays current only while its declared conditions hold. This defines no decay subsystem or evidence store.
