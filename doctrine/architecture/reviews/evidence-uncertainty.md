# Evidence & uncertainty review

Use when evidence could change a candidate ranking, risk treatment, or acceptance claim. Inputs: frozen acceptance IDs, candidate/scenario IDs, provenance, strength grade, limits, & state binding. Outputs: evidence grade, uncertainty disposition, cheapest discriminating next observation, & affected decision IDs.

Do not use for decorative citations, already-settled local implementation, or broad research under `SUFFICIENT`. Grade A is directly applicable measurement, proof, or authority; B is bounded model/prototype; C is analogous/expert support; D is unverified assumption; E is unknown or contradictory. Provenance & grade remain separate. Stop when more information cannot change a decision or treatment.
