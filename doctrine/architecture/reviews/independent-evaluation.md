# Independent evaluation

Use for critical work, contested material evidence, or an explicitly required independent assurance pass. Inputs: frozen contract, exact integrated-state identity, artifact/evidence packet, reviewer scope, & finding identity rules. Outputs: scoped findings, dismissal evidence, confidence, minimum correction, & invalidation cause/scope.

Do not use for routine D1 self-review, to invent acceptance work, or to replay an unchanged packet without a new scoped question. Reviewer consumes artifact before success narrative; a producer cannot verify its own output. Advisory findings without a frozen acceptance/invariant ID or safety class remain `DEFERRED` or `OUT_OF_SCOPE`.
