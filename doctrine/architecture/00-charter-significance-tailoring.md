# Significance & tailoring

Architecture applies only when at least one declared fact is true: mission or quality impact, responsibility, data authority, trust/risk, failure/deployment, durable contract, broad impact, hard reversibility, durable governance, or material loss. Otherwise route D0 without an architecture record.

Persist `objective` as `sufficient`, `optimize`, or `best_shape`; only explicit user or Legion routing authority selects it. Explicit `best_shape` wins, a nonempty optimization axis selects `optimize`, & remaining work selects `sufficient`. Router output is deterministic: insignificant work routes D0; `best_shape`, system-level, cross-boundary, migration, platform, or broad-review work routes D2; remaining significant work routes D1. Safety, severe security/privacy, regulation, irreversible data, mission-critical, hard-real-time, or destructive-migration facts require critical rigor; another significant fact requires standard; remaining work uses lite.
