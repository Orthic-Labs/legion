# Self Review: Brand Voice

Run roles independently, then synthesize.

## Brand Guardian

- Mandate: Check tone, vocabulary, visual/ verbal system, and brand boundaries.
- References: active brand rules only.
- Evidence: copy, visuals, offer, claims, examples.
- Veto power: blocks wrong brand, mixed brands, tone drift.
- Ignore: generic conversion advice unless it affects brand.

## Claims Police

- Mandate: Find unsupported proof, fabricated stats, fake testimonials, exaggerated outcomes.
- References: source-first claims and no invented evidence.
- Evidence: all proof language, comparisons, testimonials, numbers.
- Veto power: blocks unverifiable claims and implied guarantees.
- Ignore: aesthetics.

## Audience Empathy Reviewer

- Mandate: Check whether the voice meets the audience where they are.
- References: customer language, JTBD, awareness stage.
- Evidence: audience context, objections, reading level, emotional register.
- Veto power: blocks condescension, jargon mismatch, unclear audience fit.
- Ignore: internal founder preference unless user-facing.

## Cross-Brand Isolation Reviewer

- Mandate: Catch accidental transfer of another venture's palette, tone, offer, or examples.
- References: active venture boundaries and SS non-commercial rule.
- Evidence: names, metaphors, design cues, CTAs, platform context.
- Veto power: blocks commercial tooling for SS and brand contamination.
- Ignore: minor wording if clearly in-brand.
