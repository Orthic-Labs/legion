# Self Review: Image

Run roles independently, then synthesize.

## Creative Director

- Mandate: Judge whether the image has a clear idea, strong focal point, and memorable composition.
- References: Dieter Rams criteria translated as usefulness, restraint, honesty; editorial art direction; peak-end impression.
- Evidence: whole image, first-read hierarchy, crop, subject, negative space.
- Veto power: blocks unclear concept, weak focal point, generic stock-like output.
- Ignore: tiny production artifacts unless they damage first read.

## Art Director

- Mandate: Find composition, lighting, color, typography, and texture problems.
- References: Gestalt, contrast, color harmony, print/display production basics.
- Evidence: edges, alignment, type, contrast, palette, material realism.
- Veto power: blocks illegible type, muddy hierarchy, mismatched lighting, broken composition.
- Ignore: business strategy and channel targeting.

## Brand Guardian

- Mandate: Check brand fit, color discipline, tone, and cross-brand contamination.
- References: active brand rules and visual system only.
- Evidence: palette, typography, symbols, claims, mood, product depiction.
- Veto power: blocks wrong brand, unsupported claims, off-system visual language.
- Ignore: personal taste outside brand fit.

## Production QA

- Mandate: Find export, print, platform, artifact, and technical readiness issues.
- References: platform specs, KDP/print constraints when relevant, legibility at final size.
- Evidence: dimensions, safe margins, resolution, compression, artifacts, text rendering.
- Veto power: blocks wrong size, low resolution, broken text, visible AI artifacts.
- Ignore: concept quality unless it affects production.

## Skeptical Viewer

- Mandate: Predict what a real viewer would misunderstand, mock, or screenshot.
- References: social-feed scan behavior and hostile screenshot test.
- Evidence: first 2 seconds of attention, odd details, uncanny elements, misleading cues.
- Veto power: blocks obvious ridicule risks or confusing promise.
- Ignore: internal process details.
