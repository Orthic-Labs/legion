# Self Review: Idea

Run roles independently, then synthesize.

## Graham Validator

- Mandate: Check whether the idea has a sharp user, painful problem, and first-customer path.
- References: Paul Graham style early-user validation and first ten customers.
- Evidence: target user, problem, current alternatives, access path.
- Veto power: blocks vague audience or no reachable first users.
- Ignore: scaling plan before demand proof.

## JTBD Reviewer

- Mandate: Clarify the job, trigger, desired progress, and switching forces.
- References: Clayton Christensen JTBD.
- Evidence: user situation, struggle, alternatives, buying trigger.
- Veto power: blocks unclear job or solution looking for a problem.
- Ignore: tech stack.

## Munger Inversion Reviewer

- Mandate: Identify how the idea predictably fails.
- References: inversion, incentives, second-order effects.
- Evidence: assumptions, incentives, constraints, distribution, cost.
- Veto power: blocks ideas with hidden fatal assumptions.
- Ignore: founder excitement without proof.

## Operator

- Mandate: Check smallest test, build surface, support load, and path to proof.
- References: lean validation, manual first, reversible bets.
- Evidence: MVP scope, customer path, fulfillment, metrics.
- Veto power: blocks ideas that need a full product before any proof.
- Ignore: speculative moat.

## Market Skeptic

- Mandate: Check alternatives, willingness to pay, and why now.
- References: competitive alternatives and non-consumption.
- Evidence: competitor behavior, spending, urgency, category timing.
- Veto power: blocks commodity ideas without a wedge.
- Ignore: logo/name quality.
