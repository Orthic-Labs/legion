# Self Review: Priority

Run roles independently, then synthesize.

## Portfolio Operator

- Mandate: Compare opportunities across ventures and capacity.
- References: bottleneck thinking, focus, operator load.
- Evidence: current ventures, active constraints, work-in-progress, dependencies.
- Veto power: blocks adding work that breaks focus or active commitments.
- Ignore: novelty without strategic value.

## Opportunity Cost Reviewer

- Mandate: Identify what gets delayed, neglected, or made worse by choosing this.
- References: Munger opportunity cost and inversion.
- Evidence: alternatives, current roadmap, cashflow, deadlines, energy.
- Veto power: blocks low-upside work that crowds out higher-upside work.
- Ignore: sunk cost.

## Cashflow Reviewer

- Mandate: Check path to revenue, margin, time-to-cash, and downside.
- References: unit economics and cash conversion discipline.
- Evidence: pricing, cost, channel, fulfillment, proof of demand.
- Veto power: blocks cash-negative distractions without strategic reason.
- Ignore: brand prestige unless it drives outcomes.

## Strategic Fit Reviewer

- Mandate: Check whether the work compounds brand, distribution, assets, or capability.
- References: Porter tradeoffs and compounding advantage.
- Evidence: defensibility, reusable assets, channel leverage, brand fit.
- Veto power: blocks one-off efforts with no compounding value.
- Ignore: easy wins that do not matter.

## Energy/Attention Reviewer

- Mandate: Check personal bandwidth, context switching, and creative energy cost.
- References: focus as scarce resource.
- Evidence: current workload, emotional drag, maintenance burden.
- Veto power: blocks work likely to create attention debt.
- Ignore: theoretical ROI if execution energy is absent.
