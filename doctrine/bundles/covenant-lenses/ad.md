# Self Review: Ad

Run roles independently, then synthesize.

## Creative Strategist

- Mandate: Check hook, offer, specificity, proof, and reason to act.
- References: Ogilvy for clarity and proof, Eugene Schwartz for awareness stage, Hormozi for value equation.
- Evidence: copy, creative, offer, product context, audience stage.
- Veto power: blocks vague promise, weak hook, unsupported claim, no offer clarity.
- Ignore: media buying mechanics unless they affect message.

## Media Buyer

- Mandate: Check audience, platform fit, budget gate, targeting, and measurement.
- References: platform-native creative, CPA/ROAS discipline, clean test design.
- Evidence: campaign objective, audience, placement, tracking, landing page.
- Veto power: blocks unmeasurable campaigns, wrong objective, audience mismatch.
- Ignore: microcopy unless it hurts conversion.

## Compliance Reviewer

- Mandate: Find policy, claims, health/finance, IP, testimonial, and disclosure risk.
- References: platform ad policies, FTC-style claim/substantiation thinking.
- Evidence: claims, imagery, testimonials, targeting, disclaimers, landing page.
- Veto power: blocks account-ban risk, false claims, missing disclosures.
- Ignore: creative taste unless it creates risk.

## Landing Page Skeptic

- Mandate: Check message match and post-click conversion path.
- References: scent match, single next action, friction reduction.
- Evidence: ad promise, landing headline, CTA, form/checkout, proof.
- Veto power: blocks broken message match or no conversion path.
- Ignore: ad account structure.

## Brand Guardian

- Mandate: Check brand fit, voice, visual system, offer posture, and cross-brand contamination.
- References: active brand rules and venture boundaries.
- Evidence: copy, creative, landing page, CTA, claims, product/category context.
- Veto power: blocks wrong-brand ads, SS commercial framing, and off-system promises.
- Ignore: platform mechanics unless they affect brand trust.

## Skeptical Buyer

- Mandate: Predict objections, confusion, distrust, and scroll-past risk.
- References: buyer psychology, proof over hype, concrete outcomes.
- Evidence: first impression, objections, price/value, credibility.
- Veto power: blocks "so what?" creative and unbelievable promises.
- Ignore: internal campaign constraints.
