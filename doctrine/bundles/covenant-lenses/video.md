# Self Review: Video

Run roles independently, then synthesize.

## Creative Director

- Mandate: Check hook, story arc, emotional payoff, and whether the video is worth watching.
- References: commercial story structure, peak-end rule, clear promise/payoff.
- Evidence: opening seconds, scene sequence, ending, call to action.
- Veto power: blocks weak hook, no payoff, wrong ending, no story.
- Ignore: minor frame artifacts unless they affect meaning.

## Editor

- Mandate: Find pacing, cut logic, continuity, audio, and rhythm problems.
- References: editorial clarity, match action, sound-picture alignment.
- Evidence: timeline, shot order, transitions, music/SFX, captions.
- Veto power: blocks dead pacing, confusing cuts, missing audio, unreadable captions.
- Ignore: campaign strategy beyond watchability.

## Continuity Supervisor

- Mandate: Find identity, prop, scale, wardrobe, lighting, camera, and product continuity breaks.
- References: continuity script supervision and product spec locking.
- Evidence: frame comparisons, reference images, product dimensions, prompt constraints.
- Veto power: blocks product morphing, scale drift, camera grammar breaks, prompt violations.
- Ignore: copywriting unless it affects continuity.

## Production QA

- Mandate: Find render defects, resolution issues, compression, unsafe margins, platform readiness.
- References: platform specs, subtitle safety, final export checks.
- Evidence: rendered file, sampled frames, waveform, duration, metadata.
- Veto power: blocks broken exports, silent audio when audio is expected, unreadable text.
- Ignore: subjective taste unless it hurts delivery.

## Skeptical Social Viewer

- Mandate: Predict what viewers would screenshot, skip, or call fake.
- References: hostile social-media-user critique and screenshot test.
- Evidence: uncanny motion, hands, faces, text, product behavior, comments risk.
- Veto power: blocks obvious ridicule risks and credibility breaks.
- Ignore: internal tool limitations.

## Brand Guardian

- Mandate: Check brand system, product promise, and landing/channel fit.
- References: active brand rules and product positioning.
- Evidence: colors, type, claims, CTA, product portrayal, platform context.
- Veto power: blocks off-brand visuals, unsupported claims, wrong product promise.
- Ignore: edit mechanics unless they harm brand perception.
